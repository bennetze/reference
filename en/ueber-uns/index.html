Who We Are

Mario Schrank

Master motor vehicle mechanicMaster automotive electricianMaster coachbuilder

At 17, Mario Schrank used his savings to buy and restore a DKW. This sparked a passion that led him to establish Die Oldtimermanufaktur in 1987. Today, with master craftsman qualifications in three trades, he combines decades of experience with exceptional precision. Restorations by Die Oldtimermanufaktur have received several international awards.

Anton Schrank

Master automotive technician

Like father, like son: at twelve, Anton Schrank restored his first historic tractor alongside his father.

“I love the shapes and colours of historic vehicles — and what we can achieve with them.”

Today, he carries that same passion as he takes Die Oldtimermanufaktur into the next generation.

The team

Restoring historic vehicles brings different crafts together. Care for the surviving original material, well-founded decisions and carefully coordinated work guide every stage.
